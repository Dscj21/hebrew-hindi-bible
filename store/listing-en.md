## Full description (≤4000)
Read the Bible in its original languages, Hebrew and Greek, with every word explained in Hindi.

Hebrew Greek Hindi Bible is a free interlinear Bible for Hindi speakers, students, pastors and anyone who wants to understand Scripture more deeply. Every Hebrew word of the Old Testament and every Greek word of the New Testament shows its Hindi meaning, Devanagari pronunciation and Strong's number, with the Hindi IRV verse underneath.

★ WORD-BY-WORD INTERLINEAR
• Complete Hebrew Old Testament (39 books) and Greek New Testament (27 books)
• Hindi meaning above each word, Devanagari pronunciation below (इब्रानी / यूनानी उच्चारण)
• Hebrew prefixes and suffixes in colour so you can see how each word is built
• Indian Revised Version (IRV) Hindi verse under every verse

★ STRONG'S LEXICON & CONCORDANCE
• Tap any word for grammar (verb stem, tense, person, gender, number) in simple Hindi
• Strong's Hebrew (H) and Greek (G) dictionary with Hindi meanings
• Concordance: see every place a word appears in the Bible

★ LISTEN
• Hear any Hebrew or Greek word, a verse, or a whole chapter
• Word-by-word highlighting while it plays
• Mini player with speed, Original / Hindi / Original + Hindi modes, auto-continue to the next chapter
• Uses your phone's voices; if a Hebrew or Greek voice isn't installed, it reads the Devanagari pronunciation in Hindi

★ LEARN EVERY DAY
• Word of the Day: a Hebrew word and a Greek word every day with meaning, pronunciation and an example verse
• Verse of the Day with a daily reminder at the time you choose
• Reading streak and best streak to build a daily habit

★ STUDY TOOLS
• Highlight verses in 5 colours and add personal notes
• Search by Hindi word, Hebrew/Greek word, Strong's number (H430, G26) or reference (यूहन्ना 3:16)
• Favourites, bookmarks and reading history
• Backup and restore your notes and highlights

★ SHARE
• Beautiful verse image cards for WhatsApp Status (9:16), chats (1:1) and posts (4:5)

★ MADE FOR EVERYONE
• English or Hindi app language
• Light, dark and sepia themes, adjustable text size
• Works offline: the whole Bible is inside the app
• No account or login; your notes stay on your phone

Ideal for Bible study, sermon preparation, Bible college students, and anyone learning Biblical Hebrew (इब्रानी) or Koine Greek (यूनानी) through Hindi (हिंदी बाइबल).
